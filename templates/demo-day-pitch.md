# Demo Day Pitch Template (3 minutes)

| Time | Section | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | **Hook** | A real person's story or a striking number. "Last winter, Maria spent 3 hours calling places to find a pantry open on Saturday." |
| 0:20–0:40 | **Problem** | Who has it, how many, why current options fail. |
| 0:40–0:55 | **Solution** | One sentence. "[App] lets anyone find verified help in under a minute — just ask." |
| 0:55–2:10 | **Live demo** | ONE happy path, rehearsed. Have a backup video ready. |
| 2:10–2:30 | **How it works** | One diagram: React → Express → Supabase → AI. Mention grounding/RAG. |
| 2:30–2:45 | **Traction / learning** | "We interviewed 6 residents. The #1 thing they asked for was…" |
| 2:45–3:00 | **Ask + next** | What you need (testers, data partner, mentor) and your next milestone. |

## Demo checklist
- [ ] Open the live URL 2 minutes early (free servers sleep)
- [ ] Zoom browser to 125%, close other tabs, silence notifications
- [ ] Backup: a 90-second screen recording
- [ ] Each teammate knows who speaks when
- [ ] Practice with a timer 3 times
