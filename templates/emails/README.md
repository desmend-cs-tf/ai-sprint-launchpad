# ✉️ Email Templates

Fill in every [bracket], read it out loud once, and keep it short. Personalize the first line — people can tell when it's copy-paste.

| # | Template | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Community partner outreach](01-community-partner-outreach.md) | Asking an org/church/nonprofit to partner or share data |
| 02 | [User interview request](02-user-interview-request.md) | Getting 15 minutes with a potential user |
| 03 | [Thank-you + follow-up](03-thank-you-follow-up.md) | Within 24 hours of any meeting |
| 04 | [Demo day / launch invite](04-demo-launch-invite.md) | Inviting people to see your project |
| 05 | [Feedback request](05-feedback-request.md) | Sharing your live link and asking for honest input |
| 06 | [Mentor ask](06-mentor-ask.md) | Asking an engineer/founder for advice |
| 07 | [Open data request](07-open-data-request.md) | Asking a city office or org for a dataset |
| 08 | [Beta tester invite](08-beta-tester-invite.md) | Recruiting a small group to try it |
| 09 | [Team weekly update](09-team-weekly-update.md) | Keeping teammates/mentors in the loop |
| 10 | [Sprint recap to supporters](10-sprint-recap-supporters.md) | Thanking sponsors/community after the sprint |
| 11 | [Asking for a warm intro](11-warm-intro-request.md) | Getting connected to someone through a friend |
| 12 | [Following up on no reply](12-polite-nudge.md) | 5–7 days after no response |

**Tips**
- Subject lines: specific beats clever. "15 min to help Pilsen families find food?" > "Quick question"
- One ask per email.
- Send Tuesday–Thursday mornings.
- Never promise what your app does until it actually does it.
