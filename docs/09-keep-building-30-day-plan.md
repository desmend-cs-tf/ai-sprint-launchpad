# 09 · Keep Building: Your 30-Day Post-Sprint Plan

The sprint proved you can build. The next 30 days decide whether your project helps a real person. Aim for **4–5 hours a week**.

## Week 1 — Own your code
- [ ] Move the project off Replit to GitHub ([guide](01-life-after-replit.md))
- [ ] Open it in Codespaces and get `npm run dev` working
- [ ] Write a README: problem, who it's for, how to run it, screenshot
- [ ] Write (or update) `specs/constitution.md` and your first `spec.md`
- [ ] Add `AGENTS.md` so your AI helper follows your rules

## Week 2 — Talk to humans
- [ ] Send 5 user-interview requests ([email template](../templates/emails/02-user-interview-request.md))
- [ ] Hold at least 3 conversations (15 minutes each). Ask about their life, not your app.
- [ ] Write down the 3 most painful problems you heard
- [ ] Update your spec. Cut anything nobody asked for.

## Week 3 — Build the smallest useful thing
- [ ] Break the updated spec into tasks (each under 30 minutes)
- [ ] Replace fake seed data with 25–40 **real, verified** records
- [ ] Build the top 2 tasks. Commit after each one.
- [ ] Add one AI feature that's grounded in your data (RAG), not free-form

## Week 4 — Ship it
- [ ] Deploy ([guide](06-deploy-free.md)) and test on a phone
- [ ] Record a 2-minute demo video (Loom or your phone)
- [ ] Share with your interviewees and ask for feedback ([template](../templates/emails/05-feedback-request.md))
- [ ] Post on LinkedIn / the CodeSquad Slack with the link
- [ ] Pick your next 30-day goal

## Weekly check-in (post in Slack every Friday)
```
🛠 Project: [name]
✅ Shipped this week: [1–3 bullets]
🧱 Stuck on: [one thing]
🎯 Next week: [one goal]
🙋 Ask: [who/what would help]
```

## Signs you're on track
- Someone who isn't your friend used it without you explaining it
- You can explain every file in your repo
- Your spec and your code agree
